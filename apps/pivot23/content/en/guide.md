# How to play PIVOT 23

PIVOT 23 tells a basketball career from start to finish. You don't need to aim shots or learn button combos: you play with decisions. This guide covers the main steps, from creating your player to the last season. The rest you'll discover by playing, one career at a time.

## 1. Create your player

On the home screen press **Start**. Pick a name, a position (point guard, shooting guard, small forward, power forward or center), an origin and a jersey number from 0 to 99. Then choose the difficulty:

- **Debut**: generous growth, rare injuries, more open playoffs. Made for learning the game.
- **Pro**: the league's normal balance, no discounts and no extra obstacles.
- **All-Star**: slow growth and tougher rosters. Awards have to be earned.
- **Legend**: injuries pile up and the playoffs are a war. Only the special ones survive.

At this point you can play the draft or simulate the whole career and watch how it goes.

## 2. Three cards, keep one

Your player's profile takes shape over ten rounds. In each round three cards appear and you keep only one. The cards shape the player's visible attributes: shooting, ball handling, passing, defense, rebounding, athleticism, strength and basketball IQ. There's no perfect combination: a clear profile, a shooter, a defender or a playmaker, often tells a more interesting story than a player who tries to do everything.

The player also has a few hidden traits, tied to character and resilience. You don't see them during the career: you only get a feeling. They are truly revealed at the end.

## 3. The road to the draft

After the cards you choose how to prepare for the draft:

- **College in the United States**: solid fundamentals and plenty of visibility. You reach the draft later, but more ready.
- **European academy**: patient growth against adult opponents and a higher basketball IQ.
- **Development league**: raw talent and minutes right away, in a professional but unstable setting.

The path doesn't guarantee which league you'll start in. You can read more about the differences in our blog article [about the three roads to the pros](/en/blog/three-roads-to-the-pros/).

## 4. The call

At the draft you only see your pick number, from first to sixtieth. A team picks you for its roster, city and long-term plan, not for the difficulty you set. Every team has a playing identity, such as a fast pace, defense or minutes for young players, and an ambition, from rebuilding to chasing the title. Knowing where you landed helps you read the seasons ahead.

## 5. The seasons

Every season leaves its mark in the **Story**, the career diary. There you'll find the games that matter, the crossroads and the choices to make. The other sections open the details:

- **Year**: the season's stats, awards and playoff run.
- **League**: standings, leaders and league awards.
- **Life**: the career curve, the choices you made, milestones and the jerseys you wore.

The playoffs are played in best-of-seven series, and every series plays out differently. In summer the market opens: you may get extension offers, offers from teams chasing a ring, or max contracts. Sometimes trade rumors arrive. You can accept the move or stay out of loyalty, but the front office can also decide without asking you.

## 6. Growth, peak and injuries

The player improves with experience, reaches a peak and then has to adapt as time goes by. The peak changes from career to career. Injuries are part of an athlete's life: some pass quickly, others change a season. We don't explain how the engine works, because every career should stay a discovery.

## 7. Retirement and the Career Card

In the final winters comes the hardest question: play one more season or leave the floor with your head high. When the career ends you get a full summary: your best year, titles, personal awards, revealed traits and the Hall of Fame verdict. The **Career Card** sums it all up on a single card.

## 8. Saves and archive

You don't need an account. Your career is saved in your browser's storage on this device, and you can resume it from the home screen. Finished careers go to the archive, which keeps eight of them. If you clear the site's data or switch devices, your saves won't be available.

## Ready to start?

[Open the game from the home page](/), choose your language among Italian, English, Spanish and Chinese, and press Start. The next career starts with you.
