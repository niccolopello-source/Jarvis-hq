# Frequently asked questions

## What is PIVOT 23?
It's a basketball career simulator. You create a young player and guide him season after season, from his first choices to retirement, making the decisions that matter.

## Which devices can I play on?
In the browser, on a computer, tablet or smartphone. There's nothing to install.

## Do I need an account?
No, you don't need to sign up to play.

## How does a career start?
You pick a name, position, origin and jersey number, then a difficulty. Your player's profile takes shape over ten rounds: in each one you pick one card out of three. Then you choose how to prepare for the draft.

## Which paths can I choose before the draft?
Three roads: college in the United States, a European academy, or the jump to a development league. Each has its own pace, opportunities and risks, and none of them guarantees which league you'll start in.

## Can I play the draft or do I have to simulate it?
Both. You can play the draft and pick the cards one by one, or simulate the career and watch how it goes.

## How many difficulty levels are there?
Four: Debut, Pro, All-Star and Legend. The higher you go, the slower the growth and the heavier the setbacks.

## Is there a strategy that always wins?
No. Every choice has pros and cons, and luck plays its part too, just like in real life. Two careers with the same decisions can turn out differently.

## How do player growth and decline work?
The player improves with experience, reaches a period of maturity and then, with age, gradually declines. The pace changes from player to player. We'd rather not reveal the details: it's part of the discovery.

## Are there injuries?
Yes. Injuries are part of an athlete's life and can affect a season or a whole career.

## Are there real teams and players?
PIVOT 23 is a fictional simulation game. Team and competition names are used for descriptive purposes only; statistics, players and results are simulated. The game isn't affiliated with any league or team, and teams are shown with geometric crests, not official logos.

## What happens when the player retires?
You get a career summary with the seasons, the awards, the turning points and the hidden traits that are only revealed at the end. You can look at the Career Card and then start a new story.

## Where are my careers saved?
In the browser storage of the device you're using. If you clear your browsing data or switch devices, your saved careers won't be available. The archive keeps your last eight finished careers.

## Can I play offline?
You need a connection to open the game. Your careers stay saved on your device either way.

## Which languages is it available in?
Italian, English, Spanish and Simplified Chinese. You can switch language from the home screen.

## Is the game updated?
Yes, regularly. We also share the main news on our Instagram profile.
