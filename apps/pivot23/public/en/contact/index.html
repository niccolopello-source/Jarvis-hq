Contact

Want to report a problem, suggest an idea or tell us about your best career? We'd love to hear from you. PIVOT 23 is an independent project, and messages from players help us understand what works and what needs to improve.

How to reach us

  • Email: [EMAIL DI CONTATTO]
  • Instagram: the official PIVOT 23 profile, also linked from the game's home screen.

If you report a problem

To help us understand what happened, please tell us:

  • the device you use, for example phone, tablet or computer;
  • the browser and, if you know it, its version;
  • the game language;
  • the steps that led to the problem and what you expected to see.

Please don't send passwords, payment details or other personal data we don't need to reply. Careers are saved only in your browser: we can't see or recover them.
