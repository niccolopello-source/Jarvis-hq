About us

PIVOT 23 is a basketball career simulator you play in your browser. It started from a simple idea: tell an athlete's sporting life as a story made of choices, not as a list of numbers. Every career begins with a kid with a dream and ends, season after season, on the day he hangs up his sneakers.

Why PIVOT 23 exists

Anyone who loves basketball knows that careers are never a straight line. Some players break out right away, some grow slowly, some are slowed down by an injury at the worst possible moment, and some find their way far from the spotlight. We wanted a game that respects this unpredictability and leaves the weight of the decisions to the player: where to play, when to change teams, how to handle the hard moments.

PIVOT 23 is not an action game and doesn't ask for quick reflexes. It is a game of decisions and storytelling. You can play it calmly, even five minutes at a time, and every run leaves a different story to remember.

The project

PIVOT 23 is an independent project run by [TITOLARE], built with care and updated regularly. We work on three principles:

  • Credibility. A player's path should feel plausible to people who really follow basketball: growth, maturity, decline and retirement have to make sense.
  • Choices that matter. Every crossroads has upsides and risks. There is no road that is always right, and finding that out is part of the fun.
  • Respect for players. A clean interface, no mandatory sign-up, and saves that stay on your device.

What you'll find on this site

The game is available in Italian, English, Spanish and Simplified Chinese. Besides the game, pivot23.com hosts a getting-started guide, answers to the most common questions and a blog with original articles about basketball, player development, statistics and game design. The articles are written for people who play, but also for anyone who wants to better understand how a sports career is built and how a simulation game is designed.

We don't reveal the inner workings of the game engine: we'd rather every career stay a discovery. Instead, we share the ideas that guide us and the basketball world that inspires us.

The name

"Pivot" is the pivot foot, the move a player uses to change direction without losing control of the ball. It felt like the right metaphor for a career: a series of turns, changes of direction and decisions made under pressure. And 23 is a jersey number that holds a special meaning for many fans in basketball history.
