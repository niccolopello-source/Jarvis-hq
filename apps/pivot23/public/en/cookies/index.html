1. Technical storage (always on, no consent needed)

PIVOT 23 sets no first-party cookies. It uses browser storage (localStorage/sessionStorage) only to run the game. Data stays on your device and is never sent.

2. Third-party advertising cookies (not active today; desktop only and consent only if turned on)

Only on desktop, and only when ads are on, the site loads Google AdSense / H5 Games Ads and Google's CMP (IAB TCF v2.3).

  • Ads are non-personalized only, but Google still uses cookies or identifiers for frequency capping, aggregated reporting and fraud / invalid-traffic prevention (https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/9007336). We therefore ask for consent before loading them.

On phones and tablets we load no ads or third-party scripts and show no banner (technical storage only) — DA VERIFICARE with the lawyer.

3. Your choices

  • First visit banner (desktop): "Accept" and "Reject" equally prominent, closing with X = reject, "Manage options" for granular choices. Scrolling is not consent. No cookie wall: the game works if you refuse.
  • After a refusal the banner is not shown again for at least 6 months unless something material changes.
  • Change your choice anytime via "Cookie preferences" [POSIZIONE DEL LINK — DA DEFINIRE once ads are on].
  • Clearing site data in your browser also deletes saved careers.
