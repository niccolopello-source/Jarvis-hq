# PIVOT 23 — Disclaimer and privacy notice (D-15) — PROPOSAL, pending legal review

Nothing in this file is shown in the game yet. It needs review by a lawyer before use (see D-015: real NBA franchise names stay in the demo; legal review required before commercial distribution).

## Short disclaimer (footer of home and final screen)

IT: «PIVOT 23 è un gioco di simulazione di fantasia. Non è affiliato, approvato o sponsorizzato dalla NBA, dall'EuroLega o da alcuna squadra o giocatore. Nomi di squadre e competizioni sono usati solo a scopo descrittivo; statistiche, giocatori e risultati sono simulati.»

EN: «PIVOT 23 is a fictional simulation game. It is not affiliated with, endorsed or sponsored by the NBA, the EuroLeague or any team or player. Team and competition names are used for descriptive purposes only; statistics, players and results are simulated.»

## Privacy notice (current build)

Facts (see PRIVACY-DATA-INVENTORY.md): the game has no account, no server, no analytics, no ads, no cookies. It stores the career, the archive, the language and two UI hints in the browser's localStorage/sessionStorage on the device. Nothing is sent to us. Vercel, as host, processes standard request logs (IP address, user agent) under its own policy.

IT: «PIVOT 23 non usa cookie, account o strumenti di analisi. La carriera, l'archivio e la lingua scelta restano salvati solo nel tuo browser, su questo dispositivo, e non ci vengono inviati. Cancellando i dati del sito li elimini. Il sito è ospitato da Vercel, che registra dati tecnici di accesso (come indirizzo IP e browser) secondo la propria informativa.»

EN: «PIVOT 23 uses no cookies, accounts or analytics. Your career, archive and language are stored only in your browser on this device and are never sent to us. Clearing site data deletes them. The site is hosted by Vercel, which logs technical access data (such as IP address and browser) under its own privacy policy.»

## Must change when

Ads, analytics, Totem tracking, accounts or cloud saves are added: the notice above becomes false and a consent mechanism (EU ePrivacy/GDPR) is required before those scripts load.
