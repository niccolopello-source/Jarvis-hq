# Copy style, PIVOT 23

This is the rule for new chrome. It does not rewrite the story pools.

## Tone

Italian is the voice of the game: second person, short, concrete. English is written as English, not as a line-by-line calque. Spanish exists in the chrome dictionary and stays out of the demo picker until the events are Spanish too.

## Terms that stay in English

Draft, All-Star, All-NBA, MVP, Finals MVP, Rookie of the Year, Defensive Player of the Year, Most Improved Player, Sixth Man of the Year, G-League, NCAA, EuroLeague, PER, PPG, RPG, APG.

## Terms in the active language

| Italian | English | Spanish |
|---|---|---|
| vita | life | vida |
| stagione / stagioni | season / seasons | temporada / temporadas |
| difficoltà | difficulty | dificultad |
| Esordio | Debut | Debut |
| Pro | Pro | Pro |
| Leggenda | Legend | Leyenda |
| picco | peak | pico |
| titoli | titles | títulos |

Difficulty names on screen follow the language. The saved id does not: `esordio`, `pro`, `allstar`, `leggenda`.

## Buttons

A button starts with a verb. `Inizia`, `Salta`, `Gioca il Draft`, `Enter the Draft`. Do not use a noun as the only label when a verb exists.

## Errors and loading

Say what happened and what is kept. The missed-save line must say the previous save is not deleted. The boot screen must keep the mark, the sentence «Avvio dell'applicazione…», and the reload link.

## What not to centralize

Event text, choice effects, and summer lines stay next to the event. Moving them into the chrome dictionary would couple the story to the UI keys.
